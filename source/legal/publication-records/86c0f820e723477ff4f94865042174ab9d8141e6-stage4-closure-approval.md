# Project Architect approval — TOS Stage 4 closure

The Project Architect closed **Stage 4 — textual boot drivers and storage** on
2026-09-30 for evidence commit `86c0f820e723477ff4f94865042174ab9d8141e6`, after
the hostile final closure audit and a full preflight on that tree: 125 of 125 gates,
the two Stage 3 measurement gates run on the clean commit with the qualified ADR-0066
observer.

## Decision, as given (translated from the Project Architect's ruling of 2026-09-30)

> **R1–R3 and Stage 4 closure.** The original numeric R1–R3 are NOT regarded as
> met, and history is not rewritten as if they were. The ADR-0103 measurement is
> accepted as valid P1 evidence: R1 = 0.00375 against the original target ≥ 0.35;
> R2 = 246.1 against ≤ 5; R3 = 223.6 against ≤ 8. The decomposition is accepted
> too: after the ordinary Level-1 defects were removed, the remaining gap is the
> architectural cost of executing a byte-at-a-time data path in the Bootstrap
> TOS Core interpreter under TCG, not a defect of IPC, the scheduler, the audit
> trail or the block-service implementation. The thresholds are not lowered to
> numbers that simply make the current implementation green; ADR-0104 records
> instead that the Stage-4 Bootstrap/TCG reference ratios are characterization and
> regression evidence, not closure conformance thresholds.
>
> **Direction.** No compiled/JIT tier, bulk `Region`/`DmaRegion` primitive,
> multi-sector `BLOCK_DEVICE_V1`, or weakening of the −56 defence for Stage 4.
> Security is not changed for a benchmark.
>
> **Patent review.** No new decision is needed:
> `stage4-patent-engineering-review-2026-09-26.md` is already the step-8 decision.
>
> **ADR-0044** stays Proposed, deferred and non-blocking.
>
> **ADR-0096**: N1, the injectivity invariant, is accepted.

## What is closed, and on what evidence

- **The original R1–R3 research targets were measured and missed**, not met:
  0.00375, 246.1 and 223.6 against 0.35, 5 and 8, at P1 on clean commit `3708ea7`
  (`docs/evidence/stage4-reference-r1-r3.json`).
- **The causes are measured and documented**: about 92 % of process time is the
  block service's interpreted per-byte loops over the −56 sentinel and the one
  forced copy; the audit trail is about 4.5 %
  (`docs/evidence/stage4-reference-decomposition.json`,
  `docs/evidence/STAGE4_PERFORMANCE_REPORT.md` §R1–R3).
- **Stage 4 accepts the measured Bootstrap/TCG baseline, and makes no
  production-performance claim.** The baseline is held against regression by
  `qemu_stage4_reference_performance` under `docs/35` §Regression policy (ADR-0104).
- **Closed by evidence** (`docs/evidence/STAGE4_CLOSURE_AUDIT.md`, 72 obligations,
  67 PASS, 5 NONCLAIM by accepted decision): the canonical textual driver's
  identity and its real I/O; MMIO, IRQ and DMA authority held only by capability;
  persistent object/state storage; capsule-to-repository linkage and verification;
  crash, reset and adversarial-device behaviour; H1–H5.
- **The patent engineering review is closed without a freedom-to-operate
  conclusion** (`stage4-patent-engineering-review-2026-09-26.md`); qualified
  counsel is deferred to the `docs/24` triggers.
- **ADR-0044 remains open on purpose** and does not block this closure.
- **ADR-0096 is accepted (N1)** and checked by `interface_schema`.

## What this closure does not claim

Hardware DMA confinement without an IOMMU; availability against a device that stops
completing; power-loss durability, `FLUSH`, crash consistency or exactly-once
writes; the repository-backed `/system` and a present system commit id, which are
Stage 5's; and performance sufficient for production storage or high-throughput
workloads.

## What follows

The next stage is **Stage 4E — Interactive console** (`docs/16`). This approval
does not start it, and it does not start Stage 5.
