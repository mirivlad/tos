# Project Architect approval — TOS Stage 4C and Stage 4D closure

On 2026-09-26 the Project Architect accepted the Stage-4 closure-preparation
audit (`docs/evidence/STAGE4_CLOSURE_AUDIT.md`) and approved formal closure of
**Stage 4C** and **Stage 4D**. Their required functional and security evidence
is present; remaining Stage-4 performance decisions do not block these two
substages. The evidence basis and limits are the audit's §§2–4, 7, 8 and 12.

**Stage 4 remains OPEN.** This approval does not close Stage 4, authorize
Stage 4E or Stage 5, or turn unmeasured performance budgets into passes.
