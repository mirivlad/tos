## Classification rules

- Authority tiers are those in `docs/38_NORMATIVE_DOCUMENT_HIERARCHY.md`.
- A proposed interface document outside the hierarchy is labelled
  **unassigned** even when its own prose calls it normative. An accepted ADR
  incorporating part of that document remains Tier 1 for the incorporated
  decision.
- `PASS` means the named requirement has current evidence. It does not imply
  that the whole stage passes.
- `BLOCKER` prevents formal Stage 1 closure.
- `DEFERRED` is real work that is not a Stage 1 closure requirement or is
  intentionally queued behind a blocker.
- `SPEC-CONFLICT` means implementation must stop at that boundary until the
  authority or contradiction is resolved.
